# PRD (Product Requirements Document)

The what and why, not the how — link to an RFC or ADR for mechanism. Conventional structure; voice softens toward product but stays engineer-adjacent — requirements and metrics are constraints, not aspirations ("p95 < 200ms", not "blazingly fast").

```markdown
# PRD: <feature>

## Problem & context
<Who hits what, and why now.>

## Goals / non-goals
<What this does, and what it explicitly doesn't.>

## Users
<Who it's for; the job they're doing.>

## Requirements
<Numbered, testable constraints.>

## Success metrics
<The measurable bar.>
```
